# AFTERNS public methodology and policy evidence

Observed 2026-10-16 through eight ordinary linked public pages, all HTTP 200. This is a read-only SSR/documentation review, not a validation of the calculations or freshness. No login, subscription, calendar action, form submission, or hidden API enumeration was performed. Source HTML, readable text, link lists, and timestamps are alongside this file; `facts.json` has structured claims and limits.

1. **Scope is presented with visible counts.** [About AFTERNS · Data Sources & Methodology](https://afterns.com/en/about) claims 1,266 US-listed stocks, every S&P 500 constituent, about 12 quarters per stock, 14,756 reports, and call coverage for 101 stocks. These are site-reported counts “current as of today,” not an audited inventory.

2. **The public provenance distinguishes SEC fundamentals from consensus/price data.** The same [About](https://afterns.com/en/about) says SEC EDGAR 10-Q/10-K XBRL supplies fundamentals; Yahoo Finance aggregates supply actual announcement dates, consensus EPS, reported EPS including non-GAAP, and adjusted closes. It claims cross-checking. That establishes a stated lineage, not an upstream license or proof each record was reconciled.

3. **Beat/miss and sample exclusions are documented, with remaining gaps.** [Consensus Estimates Explained | How Accurate Analyst EPS Forecasts Really Are](https://afterns.com/en/guide/earnings-consensus) defines actual EPS above consensus as a beat and below as a miss. It says adjusted actual and consensus EPS are compared on the same basis and never mixed with SEC GAAP EPS. Its 2025-09-01–2026-08-31 study excludes 79 releases without consensus: 4,926 of 5,005 remain. Its accuracy-error formula is `abs(actual − consensus) / abs(consensus) × 100`, with medians because near-zero estimates distort means. The reviewed pages do not specify exact equality/tolerance, zero-denominator handling, revision cutoff, or a product-wide missing-data algorithm; do not generalize the article's exclusion policy beyond its sample.

4. **Reaction timing has a usable explicit definition.** [About](https://afterns.com/en/about): “percent change from the last close before the announcement to the first close after it,” same rule for BMO and AMC. The [Guide](https://afterns.com/en/guide) clarifies BMO uses the same day's close and AMC the next session's close. About defines five-day drift from the pre-announcement close to the fifth trading-day close, excess as same-day SPY subtracted, and move intensity against trailing 63-day daily volatility. Unknown/intraday timing, price gaps, corporate-action details and volatility estimator are not fully specified. NVDA's history footnote is less precise about the five-day base than About.

5. **Historical denominators and empty cases are exposed.** [NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) Earnings Date & Time · Earnings Call · Stock Reaction After Earnings](https://afterns.com/en/stock/NVDA) visibly renders 5/12 up (42%), 7/12 beats fell, +0.8% average over 12 beats, and “— / never missed” for the missing-case statistic, with a 12-report timeline. [About](https://afterns.com/en/about) says up-probability stats are hidden below four sampled quarters. This is historical sample transparency; it supplies neither confidence intervals nor proof of prediction or causal attribution to guidance.

6. **Freshness is a claim with stages, not a measured SLA.** [About](https://afterns.com/en/about) claims EPS/revenue shortly after release, AMC figures within hours that evening, call summaries a few hours after the call, and permanent price reaction after the next session's close. The text acknowledges tentative upcoming dates. This research does not establish actual latency, per-field as-of timestamps, or an upstream refresh guarantee.

7. **Calendar date certainty has an important scope ambiguity.** [US Stock Earnings Calendar](https://afterns.com/en/calendar) says displayed dates are ET, estimates are based on past reporting cadence, and unconfirmed estimates have dashed borders. [US Earnings Calendar Subscription · Auto Alerts for Report Dates](https://afterns.com/en/guide/earnings-calendar) instead advertises “Confirmed dates only” for subscriptions, local session times, automatic updates and an evening-before reminder. [NVDA](https://afterns.com/en/stock/NVDA) explicitly cautions its next date is from a calendar and may not be company-confirmed; it renders ET and KST. Display and subscription feed may have different scopes. Neither feed contents nor timezone/refresh behavior was tested.

8. **Account requirements vary across public copy.** [Calendar](https://afterns.com/en/calendar) says an anonymous user can select one preset for free, while login allows combined presets and up to 50 tickers. The [calendar guide](https://afterns.com/en/guide/earnings-calendar) also says free and no login, with a personal watchlist convenience after login. [About](https://afterns.com/en/about) more broadly says calendar subscription requires an account. This is a documentation inconsistency, not evidence of runtime behavior.

9. **Call summaries show attribution but limited traceability in sampled SSR.** [NVDA](https://afterns.com/en/stock/NVDA) has a call headline, named management quotations, positives/concerns, and “Based on the Aug 26, 2026 earnings-call transcript. Quotes are verbatim.” No external transcript link was found among this page's anchors. Quote fidelity was not checked. No “AI summary” or “AI-generated” label was found in the sampled page's SSR text/attributes; client-only labels or backend AI remain possible. About says calls include original English and Korean translation.

10. **Public-source attribution does not itself grant reuse.** [Terms of Service](https://afterns.com/en/terms), updated 2026-08-26, says data may be delayed/erroneous and has no accuracy guarantee. The reviewed short terms contain no explicit license to copy or redistribute site data/content. [Privacy Policy](https://afterns.com/en/privacy) repeats SEC EDGAR/Yahoo Finance sources. This establishes no permission conclusion either way; it is not legal advice. Robots allowance is separate from licensing.

Safe product-research inspirations: reveal sample sizes; distinguish unavailable cases from zero; name release session and timezone; expose date certainty; label basis and source close to a metric; attach source dates to summaries. These are general information-design ideas, not a proposal to copy AFTERNS data/content or change PICK/LAB selection, records, formulas, scoring, or return tracking.
